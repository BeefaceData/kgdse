Competency questions (drive the model, the website and the paper's validation)
CQ1  Which datasets have been used for subtask X, by which studies, and where is the evidence?
CQ2  For dataset D: in which categories and roles has it been used, by whom, and which variants exist?
CQ3  Which datasets have author-reported dataset limitations (e.g. leakage, label error)?
CQ4  Which dataset uses do not report a version?
CQ5  Which datasets were introduced in the corpus and later reused by other studies in it?
CQ6  Which categories rely on fewer than k distinct datasets?
CQ7  Which datasets are closed or proprietary?
CQ8  Where can dataset D be obtained (repository or DOI link)?
